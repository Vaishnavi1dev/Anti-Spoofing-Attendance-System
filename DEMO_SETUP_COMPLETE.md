**Teacher (teacher@classroom.com)**
- Dashboard with statistics
- Student management
- Attendance tracking
- Analytics and reports
- Suspicious activity monitoring

**Student (student@classroom.com)**
- Personal attendance history
- View own statistics
- Profile settings
- Limited to own data only
